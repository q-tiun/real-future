Where Real Goes Next

Barry · June 25, 2026 - 10:00pm
Been thinking about this app a lot lately, so figured I'd lay it out the way the trade desk does — connecting what's already shipped to what people keep asking for.
— Tennis is the obvious gap

Real's up to nine sports now, and the App Store reviews have the same request showing up over and over: people want tennis. Grand Slams pull a real global audience, and the whole pitch here — discuss, predict, collect, live — fits a sport built on long matches and constant momentum swings.

Wouldn't be shocked if this is next on the list, even if it launches soft, the way FC did back in 2024.

Fan Request New Sport
— The marketplace keeps getting sharper

The counter-offer feature that just shipped looks small on paper, but it's the kind of move that tells you where the trading economy is headed — less take-it-or-leave-it listings, more actual negotiation. Pair that with the new rarity filters from the same update, and it feels like they're building toward a more serious secondary market.

If that trend holds, expect more granular filtering — by sport, by season, by price range — before the year's out.

Marketplace Already Shipped
— Predictions are still young

Futures markets and portfolio tracking only landed a few months back. Once that's run a full season, I'd bet it expands — more sports getting futures support, maybe public leaderboards for the sharpest predictors. The infrastructure's already there. It's a matter of when they build on top of it, not if.
