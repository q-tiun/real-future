Where Real Goes Next

Been thinking about this app a lot lately, so I figured I’d break down some of what’s coming next based on what’s already been confirmed and what’s currently on the roadmap.
— Tennis is the obvious gap

Real’s up to eleven sports now, and one request keeps coming up over and over: tennis. Grand Slams bring in a huge global audience, and the whole Real experience — discuss, predict, collect, live — fits tennis perfectly with all the momentum swings and long-form matches.

Tennis is on the roadmap, and it feels like one of the most natural additions to the platform.

Requested by Users New Sport
— NFL Historical

One of the bigger long-term upgrades is historical NFL data, which now goes all the way back to the 2001–02 season. That’s a pretty major expansion because it opens the door for a lot more than just stats.

With that much historical coverage in place, NFL cards feel like a very realistic next step eventually — especially for iconic seasons, iconic performances, legendary plays, and throwback moments.

The deeper the historical data gets, the more possibilities open up for collectibles, challenges, and nostalgia-driven content.

NFL History Foundation Built
— Mystic / Iconic adjustments

One of the biggest ongoing discussions in the community has been card rarity — especially around plays that many users feel deserve Mystic or Iconic instead of landing as Legendary.

The devs have made it clear rarity decisions are still actively being reviewed, with some performances already on the backlog for possible upgrades.

As more edge cases pop up, expect continued tweaks to how standout performances are classified.
