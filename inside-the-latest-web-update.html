Inside the Latest Web Update

JUNE 27, 2026 · 5:30 PM
Been looking through the latest web update, and while none of the changes are massive on their own, together they add some pretty useful quality-of-life improvements.
— Earnings visibility got better

When you click on a player pass and check earnings, you can now see earnings based on the level that pass is currently at, like Iconic 1.

This update applies to player passes, team cards, and UFC cards.

It’s a small change, but it gives much better clarity on what your assets are actually earning at their current level.

Player Passes Earnings
— OTD calendar adds better historical tracking

Another useful addition is the new On This Day calendar.

You can now see your daily earnings displayed directly on a calendar, along with your total earnings for the month at the top.

That makes it much easier to look back and spot strong or weak stretches.

Instead of only seeing one total number, you can now see exactly how much you earned each day and identify patterns over time.

Were there certain days where earnings spiked? Were there slow stretches? How consistent have your returns actually been?

That kind of visibility makes historical tracking much better.

OTD Calendar
— Prediction tracking got deeper too

The prediction calendar also got improvements.

You can click on individual days to see the predictions you made on that specific date.

That makes reviewing past decisions much easier.

Bad day? You can go back and figure out whether it was bad picks, too much volume, or simply variance.

You can also customize different time ranges to analyze performance over specific stretches.

Predictions Tracking
Nothing game-changing on the surface, but features like these make the platform easier to analyze and give users more insight into their own performance and earnings.
