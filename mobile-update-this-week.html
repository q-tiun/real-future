Mobile Update Looks Likely This Week

June 28, 2026 - 6:00pm
Hearing from a dev that most of the team's recent work has gone into bringing web features to mobile, alongside continued World Cup development. An update is reportedly up for approval tomorrow - making this week a realistic target.
- Earnings get clearer

Earnings will show based on a pass's exact level (Iconic 1, etc.) instead of estimating - across player passes, team cards, and UFC cards.

- Prediction tracking, leveled up

A calendar view for predictions - see which days you finished positive or negative, click into specific days, set custom ranges to spot streaks.

- OTD calendar

A separate on-this-day calendar is also landing - it'll show how much you've earned for each day of the month, similar in style to the prediction calendar.

- Fantasy tools - not in this one, but on the radar

Separately, a user pitched better fantasy football tools - plug in your team, get recommended pickups and roster suggestions. A dev called it high priority, but this isn't part of this week's update - it's on the roadmap for later.

This isn't a flashy headline update, but it's exactly what a lot of users have been asking for. If it actually lands this week, it's a real one.
