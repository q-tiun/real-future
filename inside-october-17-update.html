Inside Real’s October 17 Update

The latest update brings Real Bot, Arena, a change to Real’s listed age requirement, and several smaller improvements across the platform.

Age Requirement Changes

Real has changed its listed age requirement from 13+ to 18+. The change is reflected in the platform’s listed age guidance; the details of how access is handled are not confirmed here.

Platform Age Requirement

Real Bot

Formerly known as Scout, Real Bot now has a full-screen experience for asking questions about players, teams, games, awards, standings, polls, and drafts.

It supports all sports, with saved conversations and shareable answers, giving users a more flexible way to explore sports information directly in Real.

Real Bot AI

Arena

Arena is a Pro-only beta that lets users compete head-to-head using players from their collections. The initial experience focuses on college football and the NFL, with more sports planned.

Arena Pro Beta

Navigation and Search Improvements

The update brings navigation changes across mobile and web, including floating navigation on mobile, improved search, a live rail, and a split-view game experience.

Navigation Search Mobile

More Improvements Across Real

Other changes include pack-opening and booster animations, projected Rax earnings, profile sharing, marketplace filters, and home-screen customization.

Sports-specific additions include NFL injury statuses, team schedule viewing options, an NBA Rookie Ladder, NHL stoppage tracking, fight-winner voting, and Three Stars after games. The update also includes performance improvements and bug fixes.

Marketplace Sports Updates Quality of Life

The Bigger Picture

Real Bot and Arena are two of the biggest additions in this update, while the navigation changes and smaller improvements touch more of the everyday experience.

The listed age requirement has also changed to 18+, making it another notable part of this release.
