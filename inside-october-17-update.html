Update Breakdown

Inside Real’s October 17 Update

OCTOBER 18, 2026

The latest update brings Real Bot, Arena, a change to Real’s age requirement, and several smaller improvements across the platform.

Age Requirement Changes 👀

Real has changed its listed age requirement from 13+ to 18+.

Real Bot

Formerly known as Scout, Real Bot now has a full-screen experience for asking questions about players, teams, games, awards, standings, polls, and drafts. It supports all sports, with saved conversations and shareable answers.

Arena

Arena is a new head-to-head game built around users’ player collections. The Pro-only beta currently supports college football and the NFL, with more sports planned.

Navigation Redesign

Navigation and search have been redesigned across mobile and web, including floating mobile navigation, improved search, a live rail, and a new split-view game experience.

Other Updates

The update adds a new pack-opening animation, brings back booster animations, shows projected Rax earnings, and improves profile sharing, marketplace filters, and home-screen customization.

Other additions include NFL injury statuses in player box scores, team schedule viewing options, the NBA Rookie Ladder, and NHL features such as stoppage tracking, fight-winner voting, and Three Stars after games. Performance improvements and critical bug fixes round out the update.

Real Bot, Arena, and the updated age requirement are among the biggest changes in this release.
